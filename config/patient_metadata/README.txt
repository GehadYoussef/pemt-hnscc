puram_tableS1_patients.tsv holds the patient characteristics of Puram et al. 2017 Cell, Table S1 (NIHMS950993-supplement-1-6.pdf, page 1), transcribed from the PDF. It is read by src/03_sc_classifier_validation/02_apply_classifier_to_sc_datasets.py. The TISCH patient IDs HN25, HN26 and HN28 are mapped to MEEI25, MEEI26 and MEEI28. The patient numbers are the same, and both IDs carry primary and lymph-node cells, as in Table S1.
